1.1.0 November 5, 2026 Latest

A cleaner, calmer Wallps

A full redesign of every screen, plus fixes that make the lock screen more reliable.

New

  • New look throughout, with native macOS controls and new type: Geist Serif for titles, Cove for everything else.
  • Previews that look like the real screens: a menu bar and Dock on the desktop, the live clock on the lock screen.
  • A swap button between the previews flips your desktop and lock screen wallpapers.
  • A status line tells you whether your wallpapers are active, ready to apply, or paused. Apply only lights up when there's something to apply.
  • The catalog has a sidebar of collections grouped by macOS release and theme, with search and counts.
  • The catalog shows which wallpapers you're using, and you can right-click any wallpaper to set it.
  • A new menu bar menu shows thumbnails of both wallpapers, and adds Swap Wallpapers, Browse Wallpapers and Pause Switching. Hold Option to reapply.
  • Right-click a preview to choose a file, browse Apple wallpapers, show it in Finder or remove it.
  • Clearer wording everywhere, including error messages that tell you what to do next.

Fixed

  • Wallps repairs a live lock screen that a macOS update removed.
  • Wallps no longer reapplies your wallpapers twice at launch, which could flicker.
  • Wallps no longer blocks logout, restart or shutdown while the catalog is open.
  • Desktop images are decoded at your display's full resolution, so they stay sharp on Retina and 5K screens.
  • The pointer no longer gets stuck as a hand after closing the catalog.

1.0.6 September 8, 2026

Liquid Glass

  • Liquid Glass styling across the app on macOS 26.
  • The settings panel no longer closes when you flip a toggle.
  • The download now runs on macOS 13 and later.
1.0.5 August 27, 2026

Live lock screens

  • Use a video or one of Apple's aerials as a moving lock screen on macOS 26 and later.
  • Live wallpapers apply faster, and reapplying one you've used before is instant.
  • Drag a file straight onto either preview.
  • The window stays open after you apply.
  • New setting to hide the Dock icon.
  • The installer now includes an Applications folder shortcut.
1.0.4 August 26, 2026

Catalog polish

  • The catalog marks which wallpapers are already downloaded.
  • A short confirmation appears when a wallpaper is applied or downloaded.
  • Apply is disabled when your wallpapers are already set.
  • Redesigned main window.
1.0.3 August 23, 2026

Apple's wallpapers, built in

  • Browse Apple's wallpapers inside Wallps. They download the first time you use them.
  • Wallps respects wallpapers you pick in System Settings.
  • Faster image loading and a new dark theme.
1.0.2 August 21, 2026

Every display, filled

  • Desktop images fill the whole display, with no black bars for 16:9 images on a MacBook.
  • External displays recover their wallpaper after the lid wakes, without replugging.
  • The app version shows in the window.
1.0.1 August 21, 2026

No more flash

  • The desktop wallpaper no longer flashes when you lock. Wallps switches before the lock screen draws.
  • A menu bar icon, reliable close-to-menu-bar, and a working Open Wallps item.

First release

  • Separate wallpapers for the desktop and lock screen.
