What is Wallps?

Wallps is a small macOS menu bar app for people who want a different image on their desktop and lock screen. macOS normally renders both surfaces from the desktop wallpaper; Wallps keeps the two choices separate and reapplies them when the Mac locks, wakes, sleeps, logs out, or restarts.

Choose a desktop image and a lock screen image, then let Wallps keep them in sync. It supports macOS 13 and later, multiple displays, drag-and-drop image selection, optional launch at login, and a menu bar control for checking or refreshing both wallpapers.

  • Use Wallps when a separate lock screen image matters to your workspace or privacy.
  • Download the free app or inspect and build the open-source project.
